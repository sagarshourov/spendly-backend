Privacy Policy

App: Spendly AI · Developer: Sagar Roy
Effective date: 17 November 2026

At a glance

Your expense records and profile settings are stored on your device. When you choose a receipt image and tap the extraction button, that image is sent to our hosted OCR service so it can read the receipt. You can use manual expense entry without sending a receipt image.

1. Who operates the app

Sagar Roy operates Spendly AI, a personal expense-tracking app. This policy describes the current app, its receipt-processing service and this website.

For privacy questions, data inquiries or concerns, contact contact@sagarroy.com.

2. Information the app uses

Information Purpose and location
Expense descriptions, amounts, categories and recorded dates Used for your expense history, totals, category budgets and spending charts. Stored locally in the app.
Profile name and selected profile photo Used to personalize the Profile and Home screens. Stored locally; the profile photo is not uploaded to the OCR service.
Language and currency preferences Used to display the app in English or Bangla and show your selected currency. Stored locally.
Receipt images you submit for extraction Sent to our hosted service after you tap the extraction button. Used to recognize text and suggest receipt amounts. A receipt may contain purchase details, store information, phone numbers or payment-related information visible in the image.
Recognized receipt text and suggested fields Returned to the app for your review. After confirmation, the net amount, description, category and recorded date are saved as an expense. This release does not attach the image or raw OCR text to the saved expense.
Service and website request metadata The hosting infrastructure may process IP addresses, request times, browser/device information, response status and diagnostic information to deliver the service and maintain reliability and security.
Support messages If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include so we can respond.

3. Camera and photo permissions

Camera access lets you photograph a receipt when you choose to do so. The system photo picker lets you choose a receipt image or profile photo. We do not need access to contacts, location or your full photo collection to provide these features. You can manage app permissions in your device settings.

Selecting a receipt does not by itself upload it. An upload happens when you request extraction. Selecting a profile photo is for local personalization.

4. Receipt processing and service providers

The OCR service uses Tesseract text-recognition software and receipt-parsing rules. We do not use a third-party generative-AI provider to process receipt images in this release. Recognition may be inaccurate; check all suggested amounts before saving.

The hosted backend and public website use Vercel. Vercel and its infrastructure providers process requests on our behalf. Processing and operational logs may occur in countries other than your own. Provider handling and log retention are governed by their applicable terms and privacy practices. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

The application code does not intentionally log receipt contents or save uploaded receipt images and OCR text to a database, object store or permanent server storage. They are processed temporarily in memory. Hosting providers may retain operational or security logs; we do not promise a specific provider log-retention period.

5. Sharing, advertising and analytics

We use information to provide expense tracking, receipt extraction, troubleshooting and support. We do not sell your personal information. This app version does not include advertising, ad targeting or a separate analytics SDK. We may disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users or others.

This website does not set application tracking cookies or use advertising analytics. Hosting infrastructure may use technical mechanisms needed to deliver and protect requests.

6. Storage and security

In a production configuration, receipt uploads use HTTPS. Local app data is kept in app storage; this release does not add its own encryption layer. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep its software updated. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

The app does not provide cloud account synchronization. However, your operating system’s backup features may include local app data depending on your device and backup settings. Manage those copies through the relevant device or backup-provider controls.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Expense records: remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app’s data. Open a transaction in Wallet and use the delete control to remove it.
  • Profile photo: use Profile → Remove photo to remove the app’s stored copy. This does not delete the original image from your photo library.
  • Name and preferences: can be changed in Profile. Clearing app storage or removing the app generally removes its local data; backups or restores may retain or restore copies depending on your operating system.
  • All local app data: use your operating system’s app-storage controls, or delete the app and manage any associated backups. Steps differ between Android and iOS.
  • Submitted receipts: the service does not intentionally persist images or raw extracted text after processing. Operational logs are handled as described above.
  • Support correspondence: retained as reasonably needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion, subject to any required retention.

This release does not create user accounts, so there is no server account to delete. We cannot directly erase information stored only on your own device. For assistance, email contact@sagarroy.com.

8. Your choices and rights

You can choose manual entry, decline receipt uploads, change permissions, correct your data and delete local records. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information we hold, or to raise a concern with a relevant authority. Contact us using the address above.

9. Children

Spendly AI is a general expense-tracking tool and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and address the concern.

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app, processing practices or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes may also be communicated within the app where appropriate.
